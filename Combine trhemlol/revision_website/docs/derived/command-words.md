# Command words

| Command word | Expected behaviour | Response pattern | Seen in source material |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| state | Provide a concise factual answer with no developed explanation unless explicitly asked. | short factual point | Paper 2 Autumn 2021 Q1(a), Paper 2 Autumn 2021 Q1(c) |
| name | Identify the correct term or item only. | label or term | Paper 2 Autumn 2022 Q1(b), Paper 2 Autumn 2022 Q2(a) |
| give | Supply one or more correct examples or items. | brief itemised answer | Paper 2 2023 Q1(b) |
| complete | Fill the missing information accurately, often in a table or diagram. | precise completion | Paper 2 2023 Q1(a), Paper 2 Autumn 2021 Q1(b) |
| describe | Set out features or process steps clearly, without needing a full judgement. | what it is or how it works | Paper 2 Autumn 2022 Q3(a), Paper 2 2023 Q5 |
| explain | Give a valid point and link it to a reason, effect, or consequence. | point plus because/so that | Paper 2 Autumn 2022 Q2(b), Paper 2 2023 Q2(a) |
| compare | Show similarities, differences, or relative suitability between options. | balanced contrast | Specification Paper 1 algorithm suitability outcomes |
| discuss | Explore multiple relevant factors in a balanced way, usually without a fully evaluative conclusion requirement. | developed multi-factor response | Paper 2 2023 Section B Q8(d) |
| evaluate | Weigh strengths, weaknesses, impacts, or trade-offs and reach a supported judgement. | balanced argument plus judgement | Paper 2 Autumn 2022 Q7, Paper 2 2023 Q7 |
| justify | Support a decision with clear reasoning tied to context and criteria. | decision plus defensible rationale | ESP planning rationale, OS risk and design decisions |
